steak Recipe



Ingredients Instructions
Steak, salt, pepper, and olive oil. 1. Preheat the grill to high heat. 2. Season the steak with salt and pepper. 3. Place the steak on the grill and cook for 4-6 minutes per side, or until desired doneness is reached. 4. Let the steak rest for a few minutes before serving.
