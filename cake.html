chocolate cake Recipe



Ingredients Instructions
Flour, sugar, eggs, butter, cocoa powder,milk and baking powder. 1. Preheat the oven to 180°C. 2. Mix all the dry ingredients. 3. Add milk and mix well. 4. Pour the mixture into a greased cake tin. 5. Bake for 30 minutes at 180°C or until a toothpick comes out clean.
